## Citation-quality checklist
- Verify all author names, initials and order from the publisher's official citation metadata.
- Confirm the year shown by the publisher; some articles have online-first and issue publication years that differ.
- Add the exact volume, issue, page range or article number where available.
- Use the citation style required by the lecturer (APA/IEEE or another style).
- A DOI is preferable to a generic webpage URL. Google Scholar and ResearchGate are discovery/access routes; the final reference should identify the paper itself.
