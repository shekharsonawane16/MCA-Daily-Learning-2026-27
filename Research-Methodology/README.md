## Important academic-integrity note
This is a **research starter pack and evidence-based draft**, not a claim that every full-text paper has been read from beginning to end. Summaries are based on publisher pages, abstracts, and full-text sections where accessible. A publisher abstract does not establish every detail of a paper's literature review, background, methodology, or conclusion. Each review marks details that must be checked in the full text. Before submitting, open and read each paper, complete the verification checklist in each review, and correct any section that differs from the full paper. Do not report an unverified number, experiment, or conclusion as fact.
