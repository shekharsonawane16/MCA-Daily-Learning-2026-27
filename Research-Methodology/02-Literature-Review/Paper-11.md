## 1. Introduction
The review notes that earlier surveys often focus on algorithms or general cybersecurity while giving less attention to feature engineering in phishing website detection.

## 2. Literature review
It consolidates studies of feature engineering for phishing URL detection and distinguishes feature categories, selection and dimensionality reduction.

## 3. Background
Feature groups include URL/lexical, HTML/content, domain, behavioural, and derived/hybrid features. Feature selection and dimensionality reduction are also discussed.

## 4. Methodology
A systematic review of recent work on feature engineering. The publisher abstract describes a categorisation of feature types and feature-selection approaches; verify the review protocol and exact included-paper count in full text.

## 5. Conclusion
The abstract identifies challenges such as changing phishing tactics, dataset limitations, high dimensionality and the trade-off between efficiency and accuracy. It points to possible work on explainability, cross-lingual/adversarial robustness and lightweight real-time systems.
