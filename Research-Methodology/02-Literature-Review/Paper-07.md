## 1. Introduction
The paper explains that phishing remains active and that attackers continually change their tactics, making effective detection important.

## 2. Literature review
It provides a broad review of phishing website detection and places proposed methods into three categories: list-based, similarity-based and machine-learning-based approaches.

## 3. Background
The paper covers phishing website detection approaches and the datasets used to evaluate them.
