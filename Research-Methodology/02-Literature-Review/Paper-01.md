## 1. Introduction
The paper frames phishing as a continuing cybersecurity problem and explains that blacklist/whitelist matching can miss newly emerging phishing URLs.

## 2. Literature review
The article surveys earlier anti-phishing methods and machine-learning solutions, comparing datasets, features, model types and evaluation practices. Read the paper's related-work section to record the exact prior surveys and citations it discusses.

## 3. Background
It introduces the phishing lifecycle and common detection families, including list-based, heuristic/rule-based and machine-learning approaches. URL structure, feature extraction and supervised classification are central concepts.

## 4. Methodology
This is a survey/review, not a new single-model experiment. It organises existing studies and compares data collection, feature extraction, modelling and performance evaluation. Verify the exact selection criteria and table-level comparisons in the full text.

## 5. Conclusion
The review emphasises that ML-based detection is an important way to identify new phishing URLs beyond known blacklists, while accuracy, false warnings, dataset quality and real-time computational cost remain important concerns. Verify the paper's exact conclusion section before submission.
