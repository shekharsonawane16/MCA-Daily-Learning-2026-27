# Paper-08: Paper Review

## Bibliographic details
- **Title:** A Systematic Literature Review on Phishing Website Detection Techniques
- **Authors:** Asadullah Safi and Satwinder Singh
- **Year:** 2023
- **Publication:** Journal of King Saud University – Computer and Information Sciences, 35(2), 590–611
- **DOI:** [10.1016/j.jksuci.2023.01.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jksuci.2023.01.004)
- **Access note:** Open-access publisher article (ScienceDirect).

## 1. Introduction
The review defines phishing as an attacker impersonating a trusted entity to obtain sensitive information and aims to compare detection techniques.

## 2. Literature review
It reviews list-based, visual-similarity, heuristic, machine-learning and deep-learning approaches. It analyses 80 scientific works according to the publisher abstract.

## 3. Background
Important concepts include URL/website detection, datasets, classifiers and the distinction between list-based, heuristic, ML and deep-learning methods.

## 4. Methodology
A systematic literature survey of 80 research items. The abstract reports that 57 of the reviewed studies used ML approaches, 31 used Random Forest, and 25 datasets were used. It also reports a CNN accuracy figure of 99.98% among the reviewed studies. These are survey-level reported counts/results, not a guarantee of performance on a common benchmark.

## 5. Conclusion
The survey reports ML as the most common approach in its selected literature and highlights the need to consider datasets and comparative performance carefully. Read the conclusion and limitations for the authors' exact interpretation.

## Verification note
Do not compare the 99.98% figure directly with results from a different dataset or paper; evaluation conditions may differ.
